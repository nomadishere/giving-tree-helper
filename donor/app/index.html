Before you start: this lends spare time on this phone, at the share you pick, to small harmless text jobs (tag, summarize, count). It never reads your files, photos, contacts, messages or accounts. No money moves. Pause or leave any time.

Honest limits: this works while the app is open on screen. iPhones and Android phones freeze web apps in the background, so closing it or locking the screen stops the helping. Best used plugged in. It uses a little battery and data.
